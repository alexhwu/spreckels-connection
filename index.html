  • Counselor weekly schedule: Ms. Ericson (Mon–Fri), Ms. Oh (Mon), Miss Elijah (Mon/Wed/Fri), Ms. Castle (Thu), Ms. Felz (Thu).
  • The counseling team is visiting every classroom to teach the Second Step Bullying Prevention Unit, which aligns with the school's No Place for Hate lessons on inclusion and being an upstander.
  • No Place for Hate Student Leaders program is now recruiting 4th and 5th graders to serve as leaders promoting kindness, inclusion, and school safety.
  • 2nd grade parents: sign up for the DayBreak "Emotion Explorers" group — a 6-week, 30-minute in-school session on emotional management and social/communication skills — by emailing Ms. Ericson at rericson@sandi.net.
  • Attendance reminder: missing just ~2 days a month (about 18 days/year) counts as chronic absenteeism and can predict lower test scores in later grades; watch the school's attendance video and review "10 Facts About School Attendance."
  • Regarding increased ICE activity near schools: Spreckels remains a safe space for all students regardless of immigration status, and federal immigration agents cannot access school facilities without a judicial warrant. Families should update emergency contacts in PowerSchool (photo ID required for pickup) and can consult the ILRC Family Preparedness Plan or request printed resources/Red Cards from the front office; contact the DEI Team at dei@spreckelspta.org for support.
  • Fund Photos family photography fundraiser: 15-minute sessions include 2 digital images for $50 (goes directly to the school) plus a $3 processing fee; additional prints available for purchase. Book a session via the link in the newsletter.
  • September is 5th grade's turn to stock the Staff Snack Shack — individually wrapped snack donations (chips, pretzels, granola bars, K-cups, etc.) can be dropped off at the front office.
  • The Wildcat Run is seeking corporate sponsors; interested businesses can contact wildcatrunsponsorship@spreckelspta.org or view the 2026 Corporate Sponsorship Packet.
  • 5th Grade Corner: a suggested Class of 2027 donation of $127 per student helps fund yearbooks, class T-shirts, Fun Day, and promotion activities.
  • 5th Grade Corner: still seeking one more Talent & Art Show coordinator volunteer — contact Erica Mittendorff at 5thgrade@spreckelspta.org.
  • Aya Expressive Arts After School program has opened a new session; see the flyer linked in the newsletter for details.
  • Governor Drive & Stadium Street curb ramp construction (2 working days, to bring ramps up to ADA compliance) is planned, likely over a weekend to minimize school impact — exact dates still TBA.
